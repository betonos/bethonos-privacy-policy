**Privacy Policy**

This privacy policy applies to all apps and games for mobile devices published by BETHONOS Wojciech Borczyk (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") on the App Store, including Pixel Miner: Idle Dig Clicker (formerly Dig Too Deep) and Horde Survivors: Bullet Hell (each hereby referred to as "Application"). The Applications are provided as a Free service and are intended for use "AS IS".

**What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?**

The Application does not collect directly identifying personal information such as name, email address, phone number, contacts, advertising identifier or precise location. Registration is not required to use the Application. The Application contains no advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

The Application uses TelemetryDeck (provider: TelemetryDeck GmbH, Von-der-Tann-Str. 54, 86159 Augsburg, Germany), a privacy-friendly analytics service, to collect anonymized usage data. The Service Provider uses it only to understand how the game is played and to improve it. The following data is collected:

- an anonymized installation ID: a random identifier created by the Application, hashed before it is sent, so it cannot be linked to you, your device or your Apple Account;
- game usage events, for example the app was launched, a run or level was started or finished, how far you got, an upgrade was bought, a character, location or achievement was unlocked, or a screen was opened;
- device model, operating system version, app version and language.

The data is not used for advertising, is not sold and is not combined with data from other apps or websites. Events recorded while the device is offline are kept on the device and sent when a connection is available. For more information about TelemetryDeck, visit [https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/).

**Game progress and settings**

Your game progress and settings are stored on your device. If an Application offers iCloud sync, your progress is also stored in your own iCloud account through Apple's iCloud service, so it can be restored on your other devices. The Service Provider has no access to this data. Deleting the Application deletes the progress stored on the device.

**In-app purchases**

If an Application offers in-app purchases, payments are processed entirely by Apple. The Service Provider never receives your payment details, name or Apple Account. The Application only sends anonymous analytics events with the outcome of a purchase or restore (for example completed, cancelled or failed) and, for a completed purchase, the product, the App Store country, the currency and an approximate price. The Application also records when a purchase offer is shown.

**Notifications**

If an Application offers notifications, they are local notifications scheduled on your device; no notification server is used. The anonymous analytics may record whether notifications were allowed and whether the game was opened from a notification.

**Game Center**

If an Application uses Apple's Game Center (for example leaderboards), Game Center is provided and operated by Apple, and your Game Center account, nickname and profile picture are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Signing in to Game Center is optional; the Application works without it. Scores are submitted directly to Game Center and are visible to other players according to your Game Center settings. The Service Provider does not run its own servers for this and does not receive your Game Center identity.

To show leaderboards, the Application loads the nicknames, profile pictures and scores of other players from Game Center and keeps them only on your device, for display (profile pictures are cached for up to 7 days). The Application asks for access to your Game Center friends list only when you choose to view friends; if you deny it, friends' pictures stored on the device are deleted. The anonymous analytics may record whether you are signed in to Game Center and which leaderboard was opened, but never your Game Center identity, nickname or scores of other players.

**Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?**

No. The Application does not collect information about the location of your mobile device.

**Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?**

Anonymized usage data is processed by TelemetryDeck GmbH, based in Germany, on servers located in the European Union, on behalf of the Service Provider. According to TelemetryDeck's documentation, this data is anonymized and cannot be traced back to individual users. No personally identifying data is shared with any third party.

**What are my opt-out rights?**

You can stop all collection of information by the Application easily by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace or network.

**Children**

The Application is not specifically directed at children. The Application does not collect personally identifying information from anyone, including children. The Applications have no registration, login or user-generated content features of their own. Where an Application uses Game Center, sign-in and nicknames are provided by Apple.

**Security**

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. The Application does not collect any personally identifying information. Anonymized analytics data is processed securely by TelemetryDeck on servers located in the European Union.

**Changes**

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time, for example when a new Application is released or an Application adds features that handle data differently. The Service Provider will publish any changes on this page before such a version is released. You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes.

This privacy policy is effective as of 2026-10-30.
